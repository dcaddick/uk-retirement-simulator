    Returns and inflation

    Cash and savings

    Household balances held outside the pensions. Interest is applied before any drawdown.

    Drawdown order

    State Pension is not a drawdown pot. When it is being paid, it counts towards the same household spending target and reduces the private-pension amount needed. The order below applies only to any remaining gap.

    Lump sum withdrawals

    Add one-off withdrawals by the oldest person’s age. They are one-off spending, not income: the amount is deducted from the selected source after growth and does not count towards funding regular household spending.

    Model assumptions and limits

    UK MVP scope

    Fictional, illustrative starter: sample values are editable and are not statistically “typical”. The simulator is a deterministic estimate, not a forecast or advice.

    What the calculation assumes

    • Salary and contributions run only before each person's retirement age.
    • Contributions are added before fixed annual private-pension growth.
    • State Pension uses the visible amount and start age entered for each person.
    • Dated lump-sum withdrawals are one-off spending: applied after growth and deducted from the selected source, but not counted as income towards regular spending drawdown.
    • Household income is cumulative: State Pension counts first, then drawdown tops up the gap.

    What it leaves out

    • UK tax, National Insurance, Pension Credit and other benefits.
    • Annuities, defined-benefit pensions and multiple private pension pots.
    • Property, investments outside pensions, detailed pension law, fees and stochastic returns.
    • Mortality, survivor behaviour and financial, tax or pension advice.
