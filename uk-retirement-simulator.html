UK Retirement Simulator

Transparent, deterministic retirement planning for a fictional household.

This educational estimate excludes tax, benefits, annuities, detailed pension law, mortality and investment risk.

Assumptions and limits

Fictional, illustrative starter: the sample values are editable and are not statistically “typical”. The simulator is a deterministic estimate, not a forecast or advice.

What the calculation assumes

  • Salary and contributions run only before each person's retirement age.
  • Contributions are added before fixed annual private-pension growth.
  • State Pension uses the visible amount and start age entered for each person.
  • Inflation increases essential and preferred spending each year.
  • Income and the visible drawdown order fund spending.

What it leaves out

  • UK tax, National Insurance, Pension Credit and other benefits.
  • Annuities, defined-benefit pensions and multiple pension pots.
  • Property and investments outside private pensions.
  • Detailed contribution limits and pension-law rules.
  • Stochastic returns, fees, mortality and survivor behaviour.
  • Financial, tax, legal or pension advice and suitability assessment.

See MODEL-METHODOLOGY.md for the full annual sequence and disclosure. No current State Pension value or age is hard-coded as official policy; those fields are user assumptions.
